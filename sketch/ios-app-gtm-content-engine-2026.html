Hook library.

Use these verbatim. The first frame carries the entire post.

Time Left

  1. Your year is already more than halfway gone.
  2. You may have fewer than 4,000 Saturdays left.
  3. I put my next big day directly on my Lock Screen.
  4. This widget stopped me saying “someday.”
  5. How many days until your next trip?
  6. A countdown should feel motivating, not stressful.

That's My Font

  1. I turned my ugly handwriting into an iPhone font.
  2. Watch this notebook page become a usable font.
  3. Your handwriting can be your phone's personality.
  4. I made a font nobody else can download.

WhatPay

  1. You are probably paying for one app you forgot.
  2. This ₹199 subscription quietly costs ₹2,388 a year.
  3. I wanted one screen for everything charging me monthly.
  4. Before buying another app, check this number.

Leave Hack

  1. Turn 8 leave days into 16 days away.
  2. Your public holidays are hiding a longer vacation.
  3. Stop spending leave days one at a time.

Feed the Fish

  1. Ten quiet seconds for your nervous system.
  2. I built a pond for the moments between meetings.
  3. No streaks. No score. Just feed the fish.

Tamil Typo + Wardrobe

  1. Tamil type should not feel like an afterthought.
  2. I turned personal Tamil lettering into something usable.
  3. You own more outfits than you remember.
  4. I built a wardrobe app because my best clothes stayed invisible.

Your first 30 days.

Four vertical videos, three X posts, two LinkedIn posts and one analytics review each week. Daily community work stays at ten minutes.

Week 1: emotional truth

Make time feel real

  • Short: “Your year is already more than halfway gone.”
  • Short: Saturday-count visual.
  • Short: next-trip countdown setup.
  • Short: Lock Screen before and after.
  • X: why you built Time Left.
  • LinkedIn: five widget designs and the final choice.
Week 2: use cases

Show specific intent

  • Shorts: birthday, exam, vacation and deadline countdowns.
  • X: honest week-one views, taps and installs.
  • LinkedIn: why one generic product page loses intent.
  • Create matching birthday and exam custom pages.
Week 3: transformation

Test That's My Font

  • Shorts: notebook to font, signature, messy writing, message demo.
  • X: the transformation GIF plus origin story.
  • LinkedIn: designing delight into a utility.
  • Compare saves and store taps against Time Left.
Week 4: double down

Remake the winner

  • Recreate the best hook with four new openings.
  • Publish one longer YouTube search video.
  • X: full 30-day numbers without polishing them.
  • LinkedIn: what content changed product decisions.
  • Choose the next six-week sprint from installs, not likes.

Small glossary.

The words are less important than the loop.

GTMHow a product reaches, wins and retains a chosen market.
ASOImproving App Store visibility and product-page conversion.
AcquisitionGetting a new person to the product.
ActivationThe first moment the user receives real value.
ConversionThe percentage moving to the next desired action.
RetentionHow many users keep receiving value over time.
