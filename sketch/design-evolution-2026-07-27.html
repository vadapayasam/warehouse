Meta Muse Spark 1.1

Meta's first paid API opens the agentic floodgates.

Muse Spark 1.1 ships a 1M-token context window and competes head-to-head with GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.8 on agentic evals. The model introduces computer-use capabilities spanning desktop, browser, and mobile, plus parallel subagent delegation for multi-step orchestration.

Chapter 02

OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol / Terra / Luna

Three-tier reasoning debuts in a restricted government preview.

OpenAI splits its flagship into three tiers: Sol (flagship with Ultra subagent mode), Terra (half the cost of GPT-5.5 at comparable quality), and Luna (fast, cost-efficient tier). Sol is priced at $5/$30 per 1M tokens and runs on Cerebras hardware at 700+ tokens per second. The model is the first to beat a public game on ARC-AGI-3, scoring 7.8%.

Cognition SWE-1.7

RL fine-tune on Kimi K2.7 pushes FrontierCode past 42%.

Cognition delivers a reinforcement-learning fine-tune of Kimi K2.7, hitting 42.3% on FrontierCode (up from 30.1%) and 1,000 tokens per second serving. At $1.97 per task, it is purpose-built for Devin and Windsurf pipelines. There is no public API.

Chapter 04

SpaceXAI Grok 4.5

First unified brand launch after xAI dissolves into SpaceXAI.

Grok 4.5 debuts under the SpaceXAI banner with a 1.5-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts architecture, trained on Cursor agent-interaction data. It scores 83.3% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 and is priced at $2/$6 per 1M tokens. The team self-disclosed Cursor codebase contamination in training data.

Chapter 05

Reve 2.1 & Seedream 5.0 Pro

Layer-based generation and precision editing redefine image AI.

Reve 2.1 hits #2 on the Text-to-Image Arena with a score of 1,306, introducing layer-based generation where users can edit one element and the image rebuilds around it. Seedream 5.0 Pro from ByteDance counters with precision editing, layer separation, dense infographic rendering, and native text support in 10+ languages at 4K resolution through the BytePlus API.

Chapter 06

Together AI Series C

$800M at $8.3B valuation signals open-model infrastructure surge.

Together AI closes an $800M Series C led by Aramco Ventures, pushing valuation to $8.3 billion. The company reports over $1B in annual bookings and open-model usage tripling year-over-year. The round underscores the accelerating enterprise shift to open-weight models and the infrastructure layer needed to serve them at scale.
