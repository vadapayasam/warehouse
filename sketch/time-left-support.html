App Support

Time Left Support

Help for Time Left, the countdown and progress tracker for birthdays, events, habits, and the stuff that keeps moving whether you like it or not.

Contact

If something is broken, confusing, or just acting weird, send a note and include the app version plus a screenshot.

Email timeleft@prabhakaran.dev

Quick Fixes

  • Force close and reopen the app.
  • Check that notifications are enabled in Settings.
  • Make sure the event date is set correctly.
  • If a widget looks stale, refresh the Home Screen.

Common Issues

  • Missing countdowns usually mean the item was deleted or not saved.
  • Widgets may lag behind for a moment after changes.
  • Cloud sync can take a short time to settle after reinstall.

Privacy

Time Left keeps your support request minimal. We only use the details you send to help fix the problem. We do not sell your data.

If you are reporting a crash, send the steps to reproduce, your iPhone model, iOS version, and whether the issue happens on the main app or in a widget.
